## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Execute Profit Taking**: Avoid "pigishness" by booking gains near peaks; follow strict exit rules regardless of emotional conviction in a stock's story.
*   **Simplify Hedging**: If bearish on the market, raise cash rather than using complex put options to hedge long positions.
*   **Maximize Compounding**: Reinvest dividends consistently to leverage compounding and facilitate dollar-cost averaging.
*   **Stage Entries**: Use incremental buying (staging) rather than full position entry to mitigate timing risk ("humility beats hubris").
