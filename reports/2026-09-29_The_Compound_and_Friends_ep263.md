*   **Correlation Myth:** Private equity is fundamentally correlated to public equity; the perceived lack of correlation is a result of reporting time lags (smoothing), not a structural hedge.

*   **Performance Dispersion:** Returns in private equity and private credit remain highly dispersed (top vs. bottom managers). This makes indexing ineffective and manager selection critical.
*   **Democratization:** The industry is moving toward lower entry barriers via tokenization and semi-liquid funds, with some minimums dropping to $500.

*   **Discount Public Alts:** Look for divergences where alternative asset managers show rising AUM and incentive fees but suffer from public market volatility/irrationality.
*   **Adjust Risk Models:** Do not treat private equity as an uncorrelated asset class in a portfolio; account for the reporting lag that masks public market volatility.
