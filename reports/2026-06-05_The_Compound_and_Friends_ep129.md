## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI Capex Evolution:** The cycle is shifting from pure chip procurement to physical infrastructure (power, cooling, electrical components), expanding the AI trade into the Industrials sector.
*   **K-Shaped Consumption:** A stark divide exists where high-net-worth individuals continue spending aggressively (e.g., luxury sports tickets, travel) while mass-market consumers are constrained.
*   **Labor Market Disconnect:** There is a noted gap between nominal compensation growth and nominal GDP; "fake news" regarding job openings may mask the actual state of hiring.
*   **Productivity Thesis:** The long-term bull case rests on an AI-driven productivity boom, though short-term risk lies in whether non-tech companies see a tangible ROI on their AI spend.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Monitor Credit Spreads:** Use credit spreads as the primary proxy for business cycle turns and systemic risk rather than lagging employment data.
*   **Pivot to "AI Industrials":** Look beyond Big Tech to electrical and power infrastructure companies (e.g., ETN, VRT) that are essential to data center build-outs.
*   **Differentiate AI Spend:** Distinguish between hyperscaler spending (defensive moat building) and enterprise spending (ROI-dependent); the latter is where the cycle is most vulnerable.
*   **Avoid Mass-Market Staples:** Favor high-end services/luxury over mid-market consumer discretionary or staples facing input cost pressures.
