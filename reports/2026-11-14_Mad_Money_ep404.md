## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Cyclical vs. Secular Growth**: Cyclicals (Mining, Oil/Gas, Industrials) are "boom and bust" and depend on economic expansion. Seculars (Consumer Staples, Healthcare) maintain consistent earnings regardless of economic health.
*   **Sector Rotation**: Institutional flows drive shifts between these two categories; secular names act as a hedge during recessions.
*   **Valuation Framework**: Focus on GARP (Growth at a Reasonable Price). A PEG ratio of $\le 1$ is considered cheap, while $\ge 2$ is prohibitively expensive.
*   **Risk Management**: Diversification is defined as limiting any single sector to $\le 20\%$ of a portfolio to prevent "annihilation" during rotations.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Utilize PEG Ratios**: Screen for stocks with a PEG of 1 or less to identify undervalued growth.
*   **Cap Sector Weighting**: Limit exposure to any single sector to 20% to mitigate systemic rotation risk.
*   **Implement Pyramid Buying**: Build positions in high-conviction stocks on price dips to improve the average cost basis.
*   **Catalyst-Driven Trading**: Distinguish between long-term investments and short-term trades; avoid the latter without a specific catalyst.
*   **Prioritize Thesis over Tax**: Avoid selling high-quality stocks solely for tax harvesting (wash sales) if the fundamental bull case remains intact.
