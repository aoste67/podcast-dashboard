## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **E-commerce Fragmentation:** The Chinese market has shifted from general marketplaces to specific niches: Content e-commerce (Douyin), Lower-tier/Value e-commerce (PDD), and High-quality e-commerce (JD/BABA).
*   **Discovery vs. Search:** A shift in consumer behavior from search-based retail (Amazon) to discovery-based, gamified "sushi conveyor belt" retail (PDD).
*   **C2M (Consumer-to-Manufacturer):** PDD's model of bypassing intermediaries to drive volume and lower prices for lower-income demographics.
*   **Regulatory & Geopolitical Risk:** Focus on "de minimis" exemptions and US-China trade tensions impacting Temu’s international scalability.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Scrutinize PDD's Cash:** Be wary of the balance sheet; a significant portion of cash may be "merchant float," representing future liabilities rather than pure liquidity.
*   **Monitor Temu Sentiment:** Track Google Trends for Temu in the US; data indicates a peak in 2023 with a subsequent decline, suggesting a potential slowdown in growth momentum.
*   **Valuation Trap Warning:** An EV/FCF of 3 is an extreme signal, but without management transparency or guidance, it may be a value trap rather than a bargain.
*   **Niche Analysis:** When analyzing retail, segment by "conversion" (high volume/low ticket) vs. "user value" (low volume/high ticket) to determine competitive moats.
