* **Sell Into Strength:** Trim winning positions during sharp rallies to lock in realized profits rather than letting unrealized gains evaporate.
* **Avoid Chasing Rallies:** Resist the urge to buy after a massive run; wait for "weakness" to find better entry prices.
* **Prioritize GAAP Earnings:** Use GAAP as the gold standard for earnings analysis to avoid management's potentially liberal non-GAAP interpretations.
* **IPO Due Diligence:** When evaluating IPOs, ensure valuations are aligned with peer groups and monitor lockup expiration dates to anticipate potential selling pressure.
* **Downside Protection:** Focus on limiting downside risk; Cramer argues that if the downside is protected, the upside will eventually take care of itself.
