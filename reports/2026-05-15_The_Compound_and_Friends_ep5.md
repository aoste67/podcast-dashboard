*   **Monitor Energy Bottlenecks**: Track electricity and power infrastructure as the next critical constraint for AI data center expansion.
*   **Apply a "Size Discount" to Mega-Caps**: When valuing NVDA, consider that its massive scale may limit further valuation premiums regardless of earnings growth.
