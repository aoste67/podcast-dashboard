*   **ETF-ization:** The trend where sector/thematic ETFs create "riptides," dragging down high-quality stocks when a peer in the same basket reports poor results.
*   **Investment Suitability:** The necessity of matching asset risk to specific goals (e.g., S&P 500 index funds for retirement vs. speculative growth for capital appreciation).
*   **AI Boom:** Continued focus on the infrastructure and software powering artificial intelligence.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Cut Losses Quickly:** Follow the mantra "your first loss is your best loss" to prevent catastrophic portfolio damage when a thesis fails.
*   **Exploit Sector Contagion:** Look for high-quality companies that are unfairly dragged down by a peer's bad earnings (e.g., buying PANW during the TENB sell-off).
*   **Ignore Macro Noise:** Avoid attempting to time the market based on recession vs. acceleration narratives; prioritize high-quality growth companies that perform in all environments.
*   **Scale Entries Gently:** When deploying cash, avoid lump-sum entries; build positions incrementally and wait for dips.
*   **Establish a Bedrock:** Prioritize low-cost S&P 500 index funds as the foundation of a portfolio before allocating to individual stocks.
