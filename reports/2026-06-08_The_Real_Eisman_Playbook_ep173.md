## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Bottleneck Rotation:** Investors are playing "bottlenecks" sequentially; capital flows move through the value chain as constraints shift from compute $\rightarrow$ memory $\rightarrow$ semi-cap $\rightarrow$ power.
*   **Agentic AI Transition:** The shift toward Agentic AI (specifically in coding) is driving real monetization and increasing the number of CPUs required per rack.
*   **The Power Gap:** A critical divergence exists where the US has chips but lacks grid power, while China has power but lacks advanced chips due to sanctions. This is driving a move toward on-site generation and SMRs (Small Modular Reactors).
*   **ROI Systemic Risk:** The "doomsday" scenario is a "shell game" where AI infrastructure fails to produce tangible returns, which would lead to a total sector collapse.
