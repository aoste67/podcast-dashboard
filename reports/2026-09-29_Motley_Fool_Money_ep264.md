## Actionable Takeaways
*   **NVDA Valuation:** Monitor the 18x forward P/E as a potential floor, but weigh this against the "circular funding" mechanism where NVDA acts as a central bank for its own customers.

*   **BNPL Risk Management:** Avoid heavy exposure to BNPL firms until the sector demonstrates resilience through a complete credit cycle.
*   **Hyperscaler Hedge:** Consider hyperscalers as a hedge against NVDA's potential growth plateau as they bring in-house silicon online.
