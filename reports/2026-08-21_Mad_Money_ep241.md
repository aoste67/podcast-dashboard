## Macro & Sector Themes
* **Cyclical vs. Secular Growth:** Cyclicals (Industrials, Materials, Automakers) depend on economic expansion; Seculars (Staples, Healthcare) maintain growth regardless of the business cycle.
* **Sector Rotation:** Institutional "hedge fund playbooks" drive market moves by rotating between these two categories based on macro health.
* **REIT Headwinds:** Higher interest rates are negatively impacting residential and multifamily REITs by reducing mortgage affordability.
* **Portfolio Diversification:** To avoid "annihilation" during sector rotations, exposure to any single sector should be capped at 20%.

## Actionable Takeaways
* **Shift Strategy by Cycle:** Rotate into secular growth names (Consumer Staples/Healthcare) when economic indicators signal a slowdown.
* **Avoid Over-Concentration:** Limit single-sector weighting to <20% to mitigate the risk of sector-wide corrections.
* **Implement Pyramid Buying:** Build positions on the way down to improve cost basis, provided the fundamental thesis remains intact.
* **Active Monitoring:** Adopt a "Buy and Homework" approach rather than "Buy and Hold," requiring continuous analysis of holdings.
* **Trade vs. Invest:** Distinguish between catalyst-driven short-term trades and long-term investment theses.
