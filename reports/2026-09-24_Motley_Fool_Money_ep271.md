## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **B2B vs. B2C AI:** Higher conviction in B2B adoption (e.g., aviation, ERP) due to predictable cadences and digitized inventory compared to erratic consumer discretionary spending.
*   **Death of the Middleman:** Agentic AI aims to remove friction by bypassing aggregators and discovery engines.
*   **Walled Gardens:** A conflict is emerging between "open" agents (Muze) and "closed" ecosystems (Amazon/Google) protecting ad-revenue data.
*   **Commoditization:** Rapid decline in the cost of AI tokens may prevent any single company from maintaining a sustainable "moat."

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Long Niche B2B AI:** Prioritize companies applying agentic AI to esoteric, high-friction industrial sectors (e.g., AAR) over broad consumer plays.
*   **Hedge Aggregators:** Reduce exposure to "middleman" platforms (UBER, EXPE) that rely on discovery-based traffic.
*   **Monitor "Walled Garden" Conflicts:** Watch for Amazon or Google blocking agent crawlers as a signal of the threat level to their ad models.
*   **Track Muze Adoption:** Use Meta's Muze app penetration as a leading indicator for the viability of agentic commerce.
