## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Social vs. Search Commerce:** A shift from destination shopping (Amazon/Shopee) to impulse-driven social commerce (TikTok Shop), which threatens low-ticket item volume.
*   **Fintech as a Moat:** Digital wallets and deposit-funded loan books create lower costs of capital and higher customer lock-in in underbanked regions.
*   **Infrastructure vs. AI:** Physical logistics and convenience infrastructure provide a durable moat that "code" or AI agents cannot easily disrupt.
*   **Emerging Market Leapfrogging:** Rapid smartphone penetration in SE Asia and Latin America allows these markets to skip the PC era, accelerating e-commerce adoption.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Quality over Valuation:** When choosing between emerging market giants, prioritize business quality (e.g., MELI's fintech moat) over cheaper valuations (e.g., SE).
*   **Monitor NPL Transparency:** For SE investors, a key catalyst/risk is the clarity of non-performing loan disclosures in their lending business.
*   **Assess "Impulse" vs. "Essential":** Evaluate e-commerce holdings by their product mix; businesses reliant on impulse purchases are more vulnerable to TikTok Shop than those selling essentials.
*   **Gaming Revenue Risk:** Be cautious of companies using a single high-margin gaming hit (like *Free Fire*) to subsidize aggressive marketplace expansion, as gaming popularity is often fleeting.
