## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Avoid Valuation Traps:** Be wary of low P/E ratios in cyclical industries, as the denominator (earnings) is often temporarily inflated at the cycle peak.
*   **Screen for "Cannibals":** Prioritize companies that aggressively reduce share count to amplify returns for remaining shareholders.
*   **Analyze Capital Efficiency:** In real estate, prioritize asset-light models over land-heavy balance sheets to mitigate downside risk during market contractions.
*   **Demand High Margin of Safety:** For highly cyclical assets, a standard valuation is insufficient; require a deep discount (e.g., 30-40%) to offset unpredictable macro volatility.
