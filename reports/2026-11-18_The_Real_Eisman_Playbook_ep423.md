* **Prioritize Raw Data over Narrative:** Ignore "story-driven" bullishness; seek out unglamorous, primary data sources (e.g., monthly regulatory filings) that the broader market is ignoring.

* **Analyze Leverage Ratios:** Use leverage ratios (e.g., 10:1 vs 40:1) as a hard metric for assessing the solvency and safety of financial institutions.
