## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI Funding Pivot:** Transition from worrying about depreciation to focusing on the "bridge" between debt issuance and data center production.
*   **Connectivity Bottlenecks:** Sector rotation toward optical networking and custom silicon (ASICs) to solve energy costs and data transfer speeds.
*   **Search Disruption:** The transition from traditional link-based search to agentic AI, creating short-term monetization headwinds for incumbents.
*   **Commoditization Risk:** The threat that frontier AI models will become commodities, shifting value from model creators to those with superior distribution or specialized hardware.

*   **Prioritize FCF over Growth:** Favor AI plays that generate their own capital (MSFT, ORCL) over those relying on debt markets.
