## Market Outlook
The luxury sector is experiencing a cyclical downturn characterized by a sharp decline in "aspirational" spending and significant headwinds in China. While ultra-high-end brands remain resilient, the broader industry is struggling with volume declines and a shift in consumer preferences toward experiences over physical goods.

## Key Stocks Discussed
*   **LVMH (LVMUY): Bearish.** Facing negative organic growth in fashion and a secular decline in wine/spirits (Gen Z shift toward tequila). High exposure to the Chinese middle class; Price Target 350 (downside) per Stig Brodersen.
*   **Hermès (RMS): Bullish.** Superior business model targeting the top 0.1% via strict gating strategies; less cyclical than peers, though valuation remains high (30x+ P/E).
*   **Richemont (CFR): Bullish.** Currently the industry's best performer, driven by strong demand for jewelry, which is viewed as a rational investment.
*   **Brunello Cuccinelli (BCNEL): Bullish.** Insulated from market struggles by ultra-high pricing and strong growth in the Americas.
*   **Kering (KER): Bearish.** Highly vulnerable to the collapse of aspirational demand and China-centric headwinds, mirroring LVMH's struggles.

## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Aspirational Buyer Fatigue:** Consumers relying on monthly paychecks rather than accumulated wealth are exiting the market, impacting entry-level luxury sales.
*   **China Wealth Effect:** Luxury spending in China is driven by property and equity values rather than GDP; the real estate crisis and deflation are primary headwinds.
*   **Category Rotation:** Jewelry is significantly outperforming fashion as high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) pivot toward hard assets.
*   **Pricing Power Paradox:** Aggressive price hikes protected short-term margins but have priced out the entry-level customer, potentially sacrificing future growth for current earnings.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Prioritize Ultra-Luxury:** Focus on brands targeting the top 0.1% (RMS, BCNEL) over conglomerates reliant on the middle class (LVMH, KER).
*   **Rotate to Jewelry:** Favor jewelry-heavy portfolios (CFR) over fashion, as precious metals are currently viewed as a safer store of value.
*   **Monitor China Lead Indicators:** Track Chinese real estate and equity markets rather than GDP to gauge the timing of a luxury sector recovery.
*   **Wait for Valuation Capitulation:** For high-quality assets like RMS, wait for a significant sentiment shift or P/E compression (e.g., toward 20x) to ensure a sufficient margin of safety.
