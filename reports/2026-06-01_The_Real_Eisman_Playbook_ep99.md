## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **The Revenue Gap:** Gary Marcus argues the industry needs ~$1.6 trillion in annual revenue to justify current spending—roughly 4x Google's best year.
*   **SaaSpocalypse:** AI is commoditizing software development, destroying subscription-based moats and making SaaS stocks volatile regardless of earnings quality.
*   **Physical Constraints:** A sector rotation toward "AI-adjacent" industrials (Power/Electrification) as energy becomes the primary binding constraint for data center expansion.
*   **Token Economics:** The transition to token-based pricing is a critical pivot point; if users reject this model, the AI investment thesis may collapse.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Avoid Traditional SaaS:** Stay away from software companies (e.g., NOW) until there is clarity on how AI disrupts their pricing power and moats.
*   **Play the Power Constraint:** Focus on infrastructure bottlenecks rather than just chips; specifically gas turbines (GEV) and electrification (ETN).
*   **Monitor Token Pricing:** Watch for the adoption of token-based revenue models in LLMs as a lead indicator for whether the bubble bursts.
*   **Hyperscaler Differentiation:** Distinguish between hyperscalers based on market tolerance for CapEx; GOOGL/AMZN currently have more leeway than META/ORCL.
