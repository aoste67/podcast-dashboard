## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI Capex Cycle:** A transition from celebrating AI investment to demanding ROI; negative FCF is now a primary trigger for sell-offs.
*   **Power as the Bottleneck:** Energy infrastructure is the critical constraint for AI scaling, favoring large-scale utilities and turbine manufacturers.
*   **M&A Surge:** Increased regulatory tolerance and high equity valuations (making all-stock deals more attractive) are driving a wave of consolidation.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Pivot to FCF:** Favor hyperscalers with disciplined spending and positive cash flow (MSFT) over those with aggressive, cash-burning capex (GOOGL).
*   **Play the Power Bottleneck:** Look at utility scale-ups (NEE, D) and energy hardware (GEV) as essential AI "picks and shovels."
*   **Monitor Photonics M&A:** Track the MRVL/LITE rumor; a closed deal likely makes COHR a strategic target for AVGO.
*   **Avoid "One and Done" Risk:** Exercise caution with infrastructure firms (STRL) that lack recurring revenue despite current AI-driven demand.
*   **Scrutinize AI Premiums:** Be wary of companies paying extreme premiums for early-stage AI labs (e.g., AMD), as these may lead to significant goodwill impairments.
