## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Turnaround Investing:** Success depends on leadership experience (CEOs who have executed turnarounds elsewhere), balance sheet solvency, and quantitative evidence over narrative.
*   **Short Selling Ethics:** Argued as a necessary mechanism to reduce the duration of mispricing and prevent more severe market crashes.
*   **Retail Sector:** High-risk environment where nominal growth may be offset by inflation; no "guaranteed winners."
*   **Private Equity/Real Estate Crowdfunding:** Viewed as high-risk due to lack of transparency, high management fees, and extreme illiquidity compared to public REITs.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Turnaround Entry Criteria:** Avoid entering at the start of a narrative; wait for several quarters of positive data (e.g., margin trends, customer growth) to confirm the trend.
*   **Leadership Audit:** Prioritize companies led by CEOs with a proven track record of executing turnarounds rather than those who simply previously ran the company.
*   **Retail Inflation Filter:** When analyzing retail "growth," adjust same-store sales against inflation to determine if the company is gaining or losing real market share.
*   **Avoid PE Crowdfunding:** Stick to publicly traded REITs for real estate exposure to maintain liquidity and transparency, noting that >50% of some crowdfunded deals fail to meet targets.
