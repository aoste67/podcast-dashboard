## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Diversify Economic Indicators:** Do not rely solely on public company earnings; track aggregate retail consumption and foot traffic to gauge the health of the mid-market economy.
*   **Monitor PPI:** Use Producer Price Index (PPI) data to identify tight inputs and cost shocks that impact the supply chain before they hit public reports.
*   **Analyze "Physical" Moats:** Look for investment opportunities in sectors that mirror the success of "Everywhere Millionaires"—tactile, essential services with high regional dominance.
*   **Correct Market Bias:** Recognize that public market data provides a "fish-eye lens" view; significant economic activity and profit generation occur in the non-disclosed private sector.
