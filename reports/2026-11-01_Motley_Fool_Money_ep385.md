* **Audit Management Agility:** When evaluating a company, assess if the leadership team is "on autopilot" or actively integrating opposing viewpoints to identify blind spots.
* **Verify AI Operationalization:** Look beyond CTO optimism; investigate if AI is being integrated into the KPIs and workflows of COOs and frontline employees.
* **Pressure Test Theses:** Actively seek out the "opposite opinion" of your current long-term investment thesis to strengthen the conviction or identify exit signals.
