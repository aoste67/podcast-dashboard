## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI Infrastructure Bottlenecks:** Data center expansion is hitting physical limits, including water scarcity, power grid constraints, and regional moratoriums (e.g., Texas).
*   **Private Credit Risk:** With $5 trillion in projected AI CapEx, private credit has replaced traditional banks; however, construction delays due to grid issues could lead to debt service failures before operational revenues materialize.
*   **Retail Divergence:** A clear shift toward value and essential retail (WMT, TJX) over discretionary mid-market brands (TGT, LULU).

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Prioritize "Energy Independent" Tech:** Favor hyperscalers with autonomous power strategies (AMZN) over those reliant on public grids.
*   **Rotate Retail Exposure:** Shift from discretionary athleisure and mid-tier retail toward discount-premium (TJX) and essential value (WMT).
*   **Monitor AI ROIC:** Be skeptical of hyperscaler valuations if consumer adoption lags behind the massive infrastructure spend.
*   **Watch LULU Proxy Fight:** Monitor the outcome of Chip Wilson's bid for board seats as a signal for potential management pivots or further volatility.
