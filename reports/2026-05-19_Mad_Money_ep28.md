## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Secular Growth Investing:** Focus on companies with the ability to scale and remain resilient during high interest rate environments or economic downturns.
*   **Critique of Index Supremacy:** Argument that index funds are a "safe" prescription from financial advisors who lack the time to manage individual portfolios for non-wealthy clients.
*   **Risk Asymmetry:** Distinction between the risk of capital loss in a bad stock versus the opportunity cost of missing a transformative (1000%+) return.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Portfolio Structure:** Adopt a 50/50 split: 50% in an index fund (e.g., SPY) for safety and 50% allocated to five individual growth stocks across diversified industries plus one non-stock hedge (gold or crypto).
*   **Position Trading:** "Trade around" positions by trimming holdings during parabolic moves to lock in gains and create liquidity for buying back at lower levels.
*   **Selection Criteria:** Target secular growth stories that demonstrate a clear path to massive scaling and can withstand macro headwinds.
*   **Portfolio Discipline:** Avoid over-diversification ("don't be a mutual fund"); sell an existing position before adding a new individual stock to the portfolio.
