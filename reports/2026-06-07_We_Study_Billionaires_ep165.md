## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Localization vs. Standardization:** Success in emerging markets requires "technical impatience" and local adaptation (e.g., cash payments, custom mapping) rather than applying a US/EU playbook.
*   **The Super App Flywheel:** Integration of ride-hailing, logistics, and fintech creates a sticky ecosystem that lowers customer acquisition costs over time.
*   **Emerging Market Risks:** High sensitivity to currency depreciation (USD reporting vs. local earnings), geopolitical instability (e.g., Strait of Hormuz oil flows), and volatile regulatory regimes.
*   **Profitability Pivot:** A sector-wide shift from subsidizing low-value transactions to targeting high-quality, convenient users to drive operating margins.
