## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI CapEx Peak**: A potential transition from aggressive spending to a plateau, impacting the entire hardware/chip supply chain.
*   **Power Constraints**: Electricity availability (transmission lines and substations) is now the "binding constraint" for AI data centers, not GPUs or capital.
*   **SaaS Disruption ("SESpocalypse")**: Traditional SaaS models are seeing revenue deceleration as AI shifts enterprise spending.
*   **Telecommunications ("Telepocalypse")**: Starlink's ambition to create a fourth terrestrial mobile service threatens AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile.
*   **Sovereign Debt**: French debt is too small to threaten global markets (BlackRock alone is larger than France), but it remains unattractive.

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Watch Hyperscaler Budgets**: Use Amazon's flat CapEx as a signal to reduce exposure to AI hardware/chip stocks if other hyperscalers follow suit.
*   **Rotate to Power Infrastructure**: Shift focus from GPU providers to power grid and energy companies (e.g., GEV, PWR) as electricity becomes the primary bottleneck for 2027+.
*   **Avoid High-Leverage AI Pure Plays**: Be cautious of companies like CoreWeave whose business models resemble specialty finance and are vulnerable to rising debt costs (currently ~15%).
*   **Monitor AI Revenue Efficiency**: Track the "revenue-to-compute expense" ratio; Anthropic's decline (2.08x to 1.70x) suggests "token maxing" has ended and pricing power is weakening.
