*   **Decision Psychology:** Discussion on "Satisficing vs. Maximizing." Evidence suggests that increasing the volume of information does not improve decision quality and often leads to analysis paralysis.
*   **Operational Constraints:** The role of boundaries in forcing priority clarification and preventing "additive bias" (the tendency to add features/commitments rather than subtract them).

## Actionable Takeaways
*   **Perform "Subtraction Audits":** Regularly identify commitments or assets to remove from a portfolio or business operation to eliminate dilution of focus.
*   **Avoid Information Overload:** Recognize that more data does not equal better decisions; rely on simple, core principles to avoid the paradox of choice in complex markets (e.g., the proliferation of ETFs).
*   **Prioritize Quality over Timing:** Adopt a "buy high, never sell" mentality for elite companies, prioritizing business quality over perfect entry points.
*   **Evaluate Early-Stage Focus:** When vetting startups, favor those solving narrow, well-defined problems over those with broad visions and excessive funding ("indigestion vs. starvation").
