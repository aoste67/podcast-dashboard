## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **Space Launch Bottleneck:** The potential retirement of Falcon 9 by 2028 creates a capacity void. Pricing power will shift to companies with operational heavy-lift vehicles; commercial satellite firms may face delays unless they redesign for lower weight.
*   **Dividend Sustainability:** Historical "dividend streaks" are now secondary to FCF payout ratios. High interest rates and debt refinancing risks make companies with >100% payout ratios prime candidates for cuts.
*   **European AI Infrastructure:** Growth is slower than in the US and more focused on "physical AI" (robotics/manufacturing). Investors are encouraged to favor established global leaders over fringe European-only plays.

## Actionable Takeaways

*   **Risk Mitigation:** Audit portfolios for "dividend aristocrats" with payout ratios exceeding 100% of FCF (specifically AMCR, GPC, and UPS).
*   **AI Strategy:** Focus on "NeoCloud" infrastructure providers (e.g., NBIS or the upcoming NScale IPO) for high-growth AI exposure in Europe.
