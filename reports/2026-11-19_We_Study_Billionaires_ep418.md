*   **The "Ecosystem Moat":** Extreme consumer lock-in (e.g., iMessage/green bubble stigma) provides a valuation floor but does not justify current multiples if growth remains in the mid-single digits.
*   **Inefficient Capital Allocation:** Critique of Apple buying back shares at 40x earnings compared to historical buybacks at 12x.
