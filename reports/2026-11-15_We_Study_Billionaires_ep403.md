## Macro & Sector Themes
*   **AI Traffic Disruption:** AI-generated search answers are eroding the "click-through" economy, forcing platforms to move toward walled gardens (logged-in experiences) to retain users.
*   **Data Monetization:** High-quality human conversation data is becoming a critical asset for LLM training, creating high-margin licensing revenue streams.
*   **Contextual vs. Behavioral Advertising:** Shift toward the value of "contextual" ads (targeting what a user is looking at *now*) over traditional behavioral tracking.
*   **Growth Deceleration Trap:** High-multiple tech stocks face severe price corrections even during "beats" if the growth rate trends downward.
