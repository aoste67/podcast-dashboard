*   **Avoid Emotional Investing:** Separate sports fandom from portfolio management; prioritize company fundamentals over team loyalty.

*   **REIT Caution:** Avoid niche REITs in high-interest environments where vacancy concessions are rising and cost of capital is elevated.
*   **Prefer Scalable Sports Models:** Focus on sports entities with global expansion potential (e.g., FWONK) rather than static franchise ownership.
